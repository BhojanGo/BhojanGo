## 1. Who we are

This notice explains how [Legal entity name — to be confirmed] ("BhojanGo", "we", "us") collects, uses and discloses personal information when you use the BhojanGo website and apps in the United States. It is also our notice at collection under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act ("CCPA").

It covers customers who order food. Kitchen partners and delivery partners receive an additional notice about the information we need to work with them; the main points are in section 5.

## 2. The information we collect

### What you give us

- **Account details** (identifiers): your name, mobile number, and your email address and password if you sign up with email (we store only a secure hash of your password, never the password itself). If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name, email address and an account identifier from them.
- **Delivery addresses**: the addresses you save or type, with their position on the map and any delivery instructions.
- **Orders** (commercial information): what you order, from which kitchen, special requests, the bill, tips, ratings and reviews.
- **Messages and reports**: quick messages to your delivery partner during an order, problem reports (with photos if you add them), and what you tell our support team.
- **Preferences**: your language, favorite kitchens and dishes, and which notifications you want.

### What we collect when you use BhojanGo

- **Location while ordering** (precise geolocation): when you choose "Use my location", your browser or phone shares its location once to find your address. We also use your IP address to suggest the nearest city we serve; this is a rough guess and is not stored with your account.
- **Device and usage data** (internet activity): your IP address, browser or app version, device type and the pages and features you use, in our server logs, to keep the service secure and working.
- **Push notification token**: if you allow notifications in the app, an identifier that lets us send them to your device.

### What we receive from others

- **Payment processor (Stripe)**: whether a payment succeeded, a payment reference, and for saved cards only the card brand, last four digits and expiration date. **We never see or store your full card number or security code.**
- **Kitchen partners and delivery partners**: updates about your order, for example that an item is unavailable or that your order was delivered (with the delivery PIN check or a photo at your door).

## 3. How we use your information

We use personal information to:

- create and secure your account and verify your phone number;
- take, prepare, deliver and track your orders;
- take payment, give refunds and prevent fraud;
- send order updates and receipts by push notification, text message and email;
- send offers, **only if you turn offers on** (they are off by default);
- help you when something goes wrong with an order;
- keep tax, accounting and consumer records the law requires;
- keep BhojanGo safe, secure and working, and fix problems.

We do not use your information for automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you, we do not build advertising profiles, and we do not use sensitive personal information for any purpose other than providing the service you asked for.

## 4. Location

- **Customers**: your location is used while you choose a delivery address and place an order. We store the addresses you save (with their map position) so you don't need to type them again. You can delete saved addresses at any time.
- **Delivery partners**: while a delivery partner is online, the BhojanGo Delivery app shares their GPS position so we can offer them nearby orders. During your delivery their position is shown to you on the tracking page. The route of a delivery is deleted one day after its last position (we keep it that long to help resolve problems); a delivery partner's last position expires within a minute of their app going offline or stopping.

## 5. Who we disclose information to

We disclose only what each party needs:

- **The kitchen partner** sees your first name, what you ordered and any instructions for the kitchen. It does not see your phone number or address.
- **The delivery partner** sees your first name and initial, and your delivery address and instructions once they are on the way to you. Quick messages between you and the delivery partner show first names only, and any phone number typed into a message is hidden automatically. Messages are hidden from both sides once the order is finished.
- **Service providers** that process information on our behalf under contracts that limit their use of it: payment processing (Stripe), text messages (Twilio), email delivery, push notifications (Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple Push Notification service) and cloud hosting.
- **Authorities**, when the law requires it, for example under a valid subpoena, court order or tax law.
- **A buyer or successor**, if BhojanGo or part of it is merged or sold, in which case this notice continues to apply to your information.

**We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.** We have not done so in the past 12 months. We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of consumers under 16.

Kitchen partners and delivery partners: we collect the details needed to approve and pay you (for kitchens: health permit, EIN, bank account and contact details; for delivery partners: identity, driving license, vehicle insurance, a background check with your consent, and a payout account through Stripe). Customers see a kitchen's name, address and ratings, and a delivery partner's first name and live position during a delivery.

## 6. Cookies and storage on your device

The BhojanGo website uses only functional cookies and browser storage that it needs to work. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics trackers.

| Name | What it does | How long |
|---|---|---|
| bg_lang | The language you chose | 1 year |
| bg_city | The city you chose with "Change city" | 1 year |
| bg_addr | The delivery address you picked, so prices and kitchens are right for it | 1 year |
| bg_hint, bg_hint_city | The country and city suggested from your IP address | 1 year |
| bhojango-session (browser storage) | Keeps you signed in | Until you sign out |
| bhojango-bag (browser storage) | The dishes in your bag | Until you order or empty it |

If you use "Sign in with Google", Google's sign-in script is loaded on the sign-in page and Google's own privacy policy applies to it. Maps on the tracking page load map images from OpenFreeMap (OpenStreetMap data); no account details are sent with them. Because we do not track you across sites, we treat a Global Privacy Control signal as a confirmation of the opt-out that already applies to you.

## 7. How long we keep information

- **Account details, addresses and preferences**: while your account is open. When you delete your account they are deleted (see section 8).
- **Orders, bills and payment records**: for at least 7 years, because tax and accounting laws require it. After account deletion they are kept without your name, phone number, email or street address. Photos you add to a problem report are kept with the order's records.
- **Support tickets**: deleted 3 years after they were resolved (we keep them that long to handle disputes and chargebacks); open tickets are kept until they are resolved.
- **Order messages** between you and your delivery partner: deleted 90 days after they were sent (until then they help with safety and support).
- **Delivery routes**: deleted one day after the delivery's last position.
- **Server logs** (which include IP addresses): kept by our hosting provider for the period its service sets, and used only to keep BhojanGo working and secure. Where a log is needed to investigate a security incident, we keep that part until the investigation ends.
- **Acceptance records** (which version of these documents you accepted, and when): as long as needed to show what you agreed to.

## 8. Your choices

- **See and download your information**: Account → Privacy & data → Download my data, on the website or in the app.
- **Correct it**: change your name, email, addresses and preferences in your account, or ask us to correct anything else.
- **Delete your account**: Account → Privacy & data → Delete my account. We delete your name, phone number, email, password, sign-in links, saved addresses, saved cards, favorites and preferences and end all your sessions. Orders and reviews stay for the legal periods above, without your personal details.
- **Notifications**: choose which order, offer and account messages you get by push, text and email in Account → Notifications. Reply STOP to any text message to stop texts.

## 9. Your state privacy rights

### California

If you live in California, you have the right to:

- **Know** what personal information we have collected about you, the categories of sources, the purposes, and the categories of third parties we disclose it to, and to get a copy of the specific pieces of information (**access** and **portability**);
- **Delete** personal information we collected from you, subject to legal exceptions (for example, records we must keep for tax);
- **Correct** inaccurate personal information;
- **Opt out of the sale or sharing** of personal information. We do not sell or share personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of, but you can still contact us to confirm it;
- **Limit the use of sensitive personal information**. We use sensitive information (account sign-in, precise location while ordering) only to provide the service, so this right does not change what we do;
- **Not be discriminated against** for exercising any of these rights.

In the past 12 months we collected these categories of personal information, for the purposes in section 3, from you, your device and the partners in section 2: identifiers; customer records (name, phone, address, payment reference); commercial information (orders); internet or other electronic network activity; precise geolocation (while ordering); audio, electronic or visual information (photos you add to a problem report); and inferences limited to your preferences (language, favorites). We disclosed them for business purposes to the categories of recipients in section 5.

### Other states

Residents of Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas, Oregon, Montana, Iowa, Delaware, Nebraska, New Hampshire, New Jersey, Tennessee, Minnesota, Maryland, Indiana, Kentucky, Rhode Island and other states with similar laws have similar rights: to confirm whether we process your personal data, to access, correct and delete it, to get a portable copy, and to opt out of targeted advertising, the sale of personal data and profiling. We do not do targeted advertising, sell personal data or profile you in ways that produce legal effects. If we decline a request, you can appeal by replying to our decision; if your appeal is denied you may contact your state attorney general.

### How to exercise your rights

- Use the tools in your account (section 8), or
- email privacy@bhojango.com, or
- write to us at [Postal address — to be confirmed].

We confirm your identity before acting, usually by a code sent to the phone number on your account. You can use an authorized agent; we will ask for proof that you gave them permission and may ask you to confirm your identity directly. We respond within 45 days, and tell you if we need up to another 45 days.

### Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

BhojanGo does not sell your personal information and does not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, so you are already opted out. You do not need to do anything. If this ever changes, we will update this notice first and give you a "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link before doing so.

## 10. Children

BhojanGo is for adults. You must be 18 or older to have an account. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 or allow anyone under 18 to open an account. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact privacy@bhojango.com and we will delete it.

## 11. Security

We protect your information with encryption in transit (HTTPS), hashed passwords, short-lived sign-in tokens, access limited by role and city for our staff, and an audit log of every change an administrator makes. If a data breach affects your personal information, we will notify you and the authorities as state law requires.

## 12. Where your information is stored

Your information is stored and processed in the United States and, through our service providers, in other countries where they operate, with safeguards that protect it as this notice describes.

## 13. Changes to this notice

When we change this notice we publish the new version here with a new version number and date and tell you in the app or on the website. Older versions remain available in the version history. We review this notice at least once a year.
