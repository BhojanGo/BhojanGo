## 1. Who we are

This notice explains how [Legal entity name — to be confirmed] ("BhojanGo", "we", "us") collects and uses personal data when you use the BhojanGo website and apps in India. We are the **Data Fiduciary** for this data under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act") and its Rules, and we also follow the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the rules made under it.

It covers customers who order food. Kitchen partners and delivery partners receive an additional notice about the data we need to work with them; the main points are summarised in section 5.

## 2. The data we collect

### What you give us

- **Account details**: your name, mobile number, and your email address and password if you sign up with email (we store only a secure hash of your password, never the password itself). If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and a Google account identifier.
- **Delivery addresses**: the addresses you save or type, with their position on the map and any delivery instructions.
- **Orders**: what you order, from which kitchen, special requests, the bill, tips, ratings and reviews.
- **Messages and reports**: quick messages to your delivery partner during an order, problem reports (with photos if you add them), and what you tell our support team.
- **Preferences**: your language, favourite kitchens and dishes, and which notifications you want.

### What we collect when you use BhojanGo

- **Location while ordering**: when you choose "Use my location", your browser or phone shares its location once to find your address. We also use your IP address to suggest the nearest city we serve; this is a rough guess and is not stored with your account.
- **Device and usage data**: your IP address, browser or app version, device type and the pages and features you use, in our server logs, to keep the service secure and working.
- **Push notification token**: if you allow notifications in the app, an identifier that lets us send them to your device.

### What we receive from others

- **Payment partner (Razorpay)**: whether a payment succeeded, a payment reference, and for saved cards only the card brand, last four digits and expiry date. **We never see or store your full card number, CVV or UPI PIN.**
- **Kitchen partners and delivery partners**: updates about your order, for example that an item is unavailable or that your order was delivered (with the delivery PIN check or a photo at your door).

## 3. Why we use your data

We use your personal data only for these purposes:

| Purpose | Data used | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Create and secure your account, verify your phone number | Account details, device data | Your consent when you sign up |
| Take, prepare, deliver and track your orders | Name, phone, address, order, location while ordering | Your consent, and to provide the service you asked for |
| Take payment, give refunds, prevent fraud | Order, payment references, device data | Your consent; legal obligations |
| Send order updates and receipts (push, SMS, email) | Phone, email, push token | Your consent (you can turn categories off) |
| Send offers | Phone, email, push token | Only if you turn offers on; off by default |
| Help you when something goes wrong | Orders, messages, reports | Your consent, to provide the service |
| Keep tax, accounting and consumer records | Orders, bills, payments | Legal obligation (GST and accounting law) |
| Keep BhojanGo safe and working | Device data, logs | Legitimate uses permitted by the DPDP Act |

We do not use your data for automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you, and we do not build advertising profiles.

## 4. Location

- **Customers**: your location is used while you choose a delivery address and place an order. We store the addresses you save (with their map position) so you don't need to type them again. You can delete saved addresses at any time.
- **Delivery partners**: while a delivery partner is online, the BhojanGo Delivery app shares their GPS position so we can offer them nearby orders. During your delivery their position is shown to you on the tracking page. The route of a delivery is deleted one day after its last position (we keep it that long to help resolve problems); a delivery partner's last position expires within a minute of their app going offline or stopping.

## 5. Who we share data with

We share only what each party needs:

- **The kitchen partner** sees your first name, what you ordered and any instructions for the kitchen. It does not see your phone number or address.
- **The delivery partner** sees your first name and initial, and your delivery address and instructions once they are on the way to you. Quick messages between you and the delivery partner show first names only, and any phone number typed into a message is hidden automatically. Messages are hidden from both sides once the order is finished.
- **Service providers** who process data on our behalf and under contract: payment processing (Razorpay), SMS (MSG91), email delivery, push notifications (Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple Push Notification service) and cloud hosting. They may use the data only to provide their service to us.
- **Authorities**, when the law requires it, for example under a valid order from a court, tax authority or law enforcement agency.
- **A buyer or successor**, if BhojanGo or part of it is merged or sold, in which case this notice continues to apply to your data.

**We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it with advertisers.**

Kitchen partners and delivery partners: we collect the details needed to approve and pay you (for kitchens: FSSAI licence, GSTIN, bank account and contact details; for delivery partners: identity, date of birth (to check the minimum age), vehicle type, driving licence, insurance, background check and payout account). Customers see a kitchen's name, address and ratings, and a delivery partner's first name and live position during a delivery.

## 6. Cookies and storage on your device

The BhojanGo website uses only functional cookies and browser storage that it needs to work. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics trackers.

| Name | What it does | How long |
|---|---|---|
| bg_lang | The language you chose | 1 year |
| bg_city | The city you chose with "Change city" | 1 year |
| bg_addr | The delivery address you picked, so prices and kitchens are right for it | 1 year |
| bg_hint, bg_hint_city | The country and city suggested from your IP address | 1 year |
| bhojango-session (browser storage) | Keeps you signed in | Until you sign out |
| bhojango-bag (browser storage) | The dishes in your bag | Until you order or empty it |

If you use "Sign in with Google", Google's sign-in script is loaded on the sign-in page and Google's own privacy policy applies to it. Maps on the tracking page load map images from OpenFreeMap (OpenStreetMap data); no account details are sent with them.

## 7. How long we keep data

- **Account details, addresses and preferences**: while your account is open. When you delete your account they are deleted (see section 8).
- **Orders, bills and payment records**: for at least 8 years from the end of the financial year, because tax and accounting laws (including the GST Act and the Companies Act) require it. After account deletion they are kept without your name, phone number, email or street address. Photos you add to a problem report are kept with the order's records.
- **Support tickets**: deleted 3 years after they were resolved (we keep them that long to handle disputes and consumer complaints); open tickets are kept until they are resolved.
- **Order messages** between you and your delivery partner: deleted 90 days after they were sent (until then they help with safety and support).
- **Delivery routes**: deleted one day after the delivery's last position.
- **Server logs** (which include IP addresses): kept by our hosting provider for the period its service sets, and used only to keep BhojanGo working and secure. Where a log is needed to investigate a security incident, we keep that part until the investigation ends.
- **Consent and acceptance records** (which version of these documents you accepted, and when): as long as needed to show we had your consent, and at least as long as the related records above.

## 8. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you have the right to:

- **Access** a summary of your personal data and how we process it, and the identities of those we have shared it with. You can download a copy of your data at any time from Account → Privacy & data → Download my data.
- **Correct and update** your data. You can change your name, email, addresses and preferences in your account, or ask us to correct anything else.
- **Erase** your data. Delete your account from Account → Privacy & data → Delete my account (on the website or in the app). We delete your name, phone number, email, password, sign-in links, saved addresses, saved cards, favourites and preferences and end all your sessions. Orders and reviews stay for the legal periods above, without your personal details.
- **Withdraw consent** at any time, as easily as you gave it, for example by turning off notifications or deleting your account. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that happened before, and we may not be able to provide the service without the data it needs.
- **Grievance redressal**: complain to our Grievance Officer (section 9).
- **Nominate** another person to exercise your rights if you die or become unable to do so. Email privacy@bhojango.com with the nominee's name and contact details.

You can exercise your rights in the app or on the website, by email to privacy@bhojango.com, or through a **Consent Manager** registered with the Data Protection Board of India once BhojanGo is connected to one. We may ask you to confirm your identity (for example with a code sent to your phone) before we act on a request. We respond as soon as possible and in any case within the time the DPDP Rules require.

## 10. Children

BhojanGo is for adults. You must be 18 or older to have an account. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children (people under 18), and we do not track children or target advertising at them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact privacy@bhojango.com and we will delete it. Any future service for children would ask for verifiable consent from a parent or lawful guardian first.

## 11. Security

We protect your data with encryption in transit (HTTPS), hashed passwords, short-lived sign-in tokens, access limited by role and city for our staff, and an audit log of every change an administrator makes. If a personal data breach happens, we will inform the Data Protection Board of India and affected users as the DPDP Act requires.

## 12. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored and processed in India and, through our service providers, in other countries where they operate. Any transfer outside India follows the DPDP Act and any restrictions the Government of India notifies.
