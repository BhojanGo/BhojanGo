Tipping is up to you, and every rupee or cent of it goes to the person who brings your food.

## Adding a tip

At checkout, in the tip section:

- pick one of the suggested amounts,
- choose **Other** and type any amount, or
- choose **None**.

Your tip shows on the fair bill as its own line, tagged **DELIVERY PARTNER**.

## Where your tip goes

- **100% to your delivery partner.** BhojanGo takes nothing from tips.
- It's paid on top of their pay for the trip, not instead of it.
- It doesn't count towards the hourly floor we guarantee delivery partners, so your tip is always extra.
- Your delivery partner sees the tip as part of the trip's pay when the delivery is offered to them.

## If your order is cancelled

If a paid order is cancelled, you get your tip back along with the rest of the order.

## Changing your tip

Your tip is part of the price held at checkout. If you change it before paying, we work out the bill again. Once you've paid, the tip is part of your order.

> The suggested tip amounts are set for each city. They're only suggestions: any amount, or none, is fine.
