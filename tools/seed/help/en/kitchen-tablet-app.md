The BhojanGo Restaurant app runs on your restaurant's tablet. New orders ring until you accept or reject them.

## New orders

Each new order shows the items, any customer note and a countdown to accept.

- **Accept** to start. The order moves to **Preparing**.
- **Reject** if you can't make it. The customer gets a full refund.

If you don't accept in time, the order is cancelled and the customer is refunded in full, so keep the tablet's sound on during opening hours.

## Cooking and handover

1. When the food is nearly done, tap **Almost ready**. This is when we start finding a delivery partner, so they arrive as the food is ready.
2. Tap **Mark ready** when it's packed. Check the order code on the bag.
3. The order leaves your screen when the delivery partner confirms pickup.

## When you're busy

- **Busy? Add 10 min**: new orders quote extra preparation time for the next hour. You can undo it.
- **Pause orders**: stop new orders for a set time or until you resume. Customers see you're paused. Orders already placed carry on.

## Menu and store

- **Sold out today**: a dish can't be ordered until your next day. It comes back automatically.
- **Store hours**: set your weekly hours, with more than one range a day if you like, plus holidays and special hours.
- **Default prep time**: how long you usually take from accepting to ready.
- Price changes are checked against your partner agreement before they go live.

## Offers you fund

Start a free delivery, percent off or amount off offer, with a weekly limit. It starts straight away, comes off your weekly payout and stops for the week when the limit is reached. You can end it at any time.

> Need help with an order, a payout or the menu? Use **Help** on the tablet to [ask us to call you back](/help/a/kitchen-call-back).
