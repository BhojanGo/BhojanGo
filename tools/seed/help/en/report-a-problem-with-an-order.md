Sorry something went wrong. You can report it yourself from the order, once it has been delivered.

## How to report

1. Open the order from [Your orders](/account/orders).
2. Choose **Report a problem**.
3. Pick the items that were affected, and how many of each.
4. Tell us what went wrong: **Missing**, **Wrong item**, **Not good**, **Spilled or damaged** or **Very late**.
5. Add a note and a photo if you like. A photo helps, but it's optional.
6. Send the report.

## What happens next

We work out what those items cost you: their price, less their share of any discount, plus the tax you paid on them.

- **Refunded at once.** If the amount is within your city's automatic refund limit for one order, we refund it straight away to the way you paid. You'll see "Refunded" on the screen.
- **Sent to support.** If it's more than that limit, a person on our support team looks at it first. You'll see "Sent to support", and we'll get back to you.

Anything you were already refunded automatically on the same order counts towards the limit.

## Good to know

- You can report a problem only after the order is delivered.
- Reports have a time limit after delivery. It's set for each city; once it has passed, the app tells you the order can no longer be reported.
- Items that were already refunded can't be refunded twice.
- Kitchens pay for their own mistakes, like a missing or wrong item. BhojanGo pays for delivery problems.

> Something this form doesn't cover? Tap **Help** on the order page and we'll take it from there.
