Running a restaurant is busy. Instead of waiting on hold, ask us to call you back from the tablet.

## Request a call back

1. On the BhojanGo Restaurant tablet, open **Help**.
2. Under **Partner support**, choose what it's about: an order, a payout, the menu, the account or something else.
3. Add a short note if it helps. It's optional.
4. Tap **Request a call back**.

The tablet shows **We'll call you back** while your request is open.

## Who calls and when

Your request goes to the BhojanGo support team for your city, with your restaurant's name and the number to call. We call the phone number the tablet signed in with, during your opening hours. Whoever calls you closes the request with a note of what was done.

## Changing your request

We keep one open request per restaurant. If something else comes up before we call, change the topic or the note and tap **Update my request**. You don't need to send a new one.

## Good to know

- If you reject an order, or it's cancelled because it wasn't accepted in time, the customer is refunded in full. You don't need to call us to arrange it.
- Questions about a payout are quicker if you have the week's statement open.

> Make sure the number your tablet signs in with is one someone in the restaurant answers.
