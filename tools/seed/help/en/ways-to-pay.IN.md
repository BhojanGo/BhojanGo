In India, BhojanGo takes payments through Razorpay, our payment partner. You pay before the kitchen gets your order, so there's nothing to settle at the door.

## Ways to pay

- **UPI.** Approve the payment in your UPI app.
- **Credit and debit cards.** Your card details are entered in Razorpay's secure form, not ours.
- **Wallets.** Pay with a supported wallet in the Razorpay checkout.

Checkout shows the ways to pay that work for your address.

## Saved cards

You can save a card to pay faster next time. BhojanGo never sees or stores your full card number: Razorpay keeps it and gives us a token, and we show only the card brand, the last 4 digits and the expiry date. You can remove a saved card or choose a default in your account.

## If a payment fails

If a payment doesn't go through, you aren't charged. Your order waits, and the price stays held for a while so you can try again with the same or another method. See [Your price is held while you pay](/help/a/price-held-while-you-pay).

If a payment is slow to confirm, the app tells you and updates your order as soon as it's confirmed.

## Refunds

Refunds go back to the way you paid: your UPI account, card or wallet. See [How do refunds work?](/help/a/how-refunds-work).

## Your bill and invoice

Your bill shows GST as its own line. After your order, you can download a tax invoice from the order's receipt.

> We never ask for your card number, UPI PIN or OTP on a call or in a message.
