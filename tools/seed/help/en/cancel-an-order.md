Yes, as long as the kitchen hasn't started on it. Every paid order that is cancelled is refunded in full, to the way you paid.

## How to cancel

Open the order's tracking page (from [Your orders](/account/orders)) and tap **Cancel order** while it's shown. Once the order can't be cancelled any more, the button goes away.

## When you can cancel

- **Before you've paid.** You can cancel at any time. Nothing has been charged.
- **Before the kitchen accepts.** You can cancel at any time, for a full refund.
- **Just after the kitchen accepts.** You still have a short window, counted from when you placed the order. The length is set for each city.
- **Once the kitchen is cooking.** You can't cancel it yourself any more. If something is wrong, contact support from the order and we'll help.

Once a delivery partner has picked up your order, it can no longer be cancelled.

## When we cancel for you

Sometimes an order is cancelled without you doing anything:

- **The payment didn't finish.** An order that is never paid is cancelled after a short while. You aren't charged.
- **The kitchen didn't accept in time.** Kitchens have a limited time to accept a new order. If they don't, we cancel it and refund you in full.
- **The kitchen couldn't take it.** If a kitchen rejects or cancels your order, for example because a dish ran out, you get a full refund.

If a payment goes through after an order was already cancelled, we refund it in full automatically.

## Your refund

A cancelled order is refunded in full: food, fees, tax and tip. It goes back to the card, UPI account or other method you paid with. How quickly it shows up depends on your bank or payment provider.

> Changed your mind about a dish but the kitchen is already cooking? We can't stop it, but you can [report a problem](/help/a/report-a-problem-with-an-order) if something arrives wrong.
