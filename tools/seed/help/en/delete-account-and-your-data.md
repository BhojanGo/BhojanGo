You're in control of your data. Both options are under **Privacy and your data** in your account.

## Download your data

Tap **Download my data** and we give you a file with everything we hold about you:

- your profile,
- your saved addresses,
- your orders and their bills,
- your saved cards, shown only by brand and last 4 digits,
- your favourites and notification settings,
- your support requests, problem reports and refunds,
- your ratings and reviews.

It contains only your own information, nothing about delivery partners, kitchen partners or other customers.

## Delete your account

1. Tap **Delete my account**.
2. Read what will happen, then confirm.

When you delete your account, we:

- remove your name, email, phone number and any linked Google sign-in,
- remove your saved addresses, saved cards, favourites and notification settings,
- sign you out on every device straight away,
- close the account.

**This can't be undone.**

## What we keep

Kitchens and tax records need past orders to stay complete, so your past orders stay in our records **without** your personal details: no name, no phone, and no street, map pin or delivery notes. Only the city, state, postal code and country stay, because they're needed for tax.

> Want to download your data first? Do it before you delete your account. Once it's deleted, you can't sign in to get it.
