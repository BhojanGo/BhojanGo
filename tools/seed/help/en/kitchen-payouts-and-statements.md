Restaurant partners are paid weekly, straight to your bank account, through our payments partner: Razorpay in India and Stripe in the US. Your statements are under **Payouts** in the BhojanGo Restaurant app.

## What you're paid for each order

For every delivered order in the week:

- **plus** the food and packaging,
- **minus** the card processing fee for that order (the payment provider's fee on what the customer paid, not counting the tip),
- **minus** BhojanGo's commission, a percentage of the food only (0% unless your agreement says otherwise),
- **minus** any offers you funded on that order,
- **minus** refunds for your own mistakes, such as a missing or wrong item or poor quality. For those, you lose the item's food value, less the commission you paid on it.

In India, if you collect a tax yourself, it's added to your payout.

## What you don't pay for

- **Delivery problems.** Refunds for food that was spilled, damaged or very late are paid by BhojanGo. They appear on your statement for information only.
- **Customer fees and tips.** Delivery, service and other customer fees go to BhojanGo, and tips go to the delivery partner. None of it comes off your payout.
- **Offers BhojanGo funds.** You're paid in full for the food.

## Payout status

- **Paid**: the payout has been sent to your bank.
- **On its way**: the payout is being sent.
- **Waiting for your bank details**: finish setting up your bank account and we pay it.
- **Didn't go through**: we'll try again.

## Checking a statement

Each weekly statement lists your orders and the amounts above, including what offers cost you that week. Every payout is checked and approved by two people on our finance team before it's sent.

> A question about a payout? Use **Help** on the tablet and choose **A payout** when you ask us to call you back.
