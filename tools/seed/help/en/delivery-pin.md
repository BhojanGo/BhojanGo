Every order gets its own 4-digit delivery PIN. It's how your delivery partner confirms they handed the food to the right person.

## Where to find it

Open your order from [Your orders](/account/orders). The PIN is on the tracking page, marked **Delivery PIN**.

## At the door

1. Your delivery partner asks you for the PIN.
2. You tell them the 4 digits.
3. They enter it in the BhojanGo Delivery app. If it matches, your order is marked delivered.

If the PIN doesn't match, the app asks them to check it with you again.

## If you're not at the door

If you've asked for your food to be left at the door, or can't come to the door, your delivery partner can take a photo of the bags where they leave them instead. The photo is the proof of delivery.

A delivery partner can't mark an order delivered without either the PIN or a photo at the door.

## Keep it for the handover

- Only give the PIN to your delivery partner when your food is in front of you.
- BhojanGo staff will never ask you for your PIN by phone.
- Each PIN works for one order only.

> Got your food but something's missing or wrong? You can [report a problem](/help/a/report-a-problem-with-an-order) from the order.
