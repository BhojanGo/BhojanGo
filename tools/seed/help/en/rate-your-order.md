Your rating helps other people choose, and helps kitchens get better.

## Rating an order

1. Open a delivered order from [Your orders](/account/orders).
2. Choose 1 to 5 stars for the kitchen.
3. Add a short comment if you'd like. It's optional.
4. Send it.

You can rate an order only after it's delivered. Each order gets one rating.

## Changing your rating

You can change your stars or comment for 7 days after you first rate the order. After that, the rating is locked.

## What kitchens and other people see

- Your stars count towards the kitchen's rating that everyone sees.
- Kitchens see your stars and comment, but not your name.
- A kitchen shows as **New** until it has 5 ratings, so one early rating doesn't decide how it looks.
- Ratings only come from delivered orders, so every rating is from someone who actually ordered there.

## When something went wrong

A rating doesn't get you a refund. If something was missing, wrong or spilled, please [report a problem](/help/a/report-a-problem-with-an-order) from the order as well, so we can put it right.

> Ratings are about the kitchen and the food. If your problem was with the delivery, tell us through Report a problem.
