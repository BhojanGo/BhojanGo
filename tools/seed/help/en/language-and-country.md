Your language and your country are separate settings. Language is how BhojanGo talks to you. Country is where your food is delivered.

## Changing your language

1. Open **Profile & settings** in your account.
2. Under **Language and country**, tap **Change** next to Language.
3. Pick a language.

Your language changes the app, the website, our messages and our emails. It doesn't change any prices.

## Languages we offer

In **India**: English, Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Marathi and Bengali.

In the **US**: English and Spanish.

Quick messages between you and your delivery partner are shown to each of you in your own language.

## Your country

Your country is set by your delivery address. To order in another country, change your delivery address to one there.

Your country decides:

- the restaurants you see,
- the currency and the prices, including fees and tax,
- the ways you can pay: Razorpay in India, Stripe in the US.

Prices are set city by city, so even within a country, fees can be different from one city to another.

> Ordering for someone in another city? Just enter their address. The restaurants, prices and fees for that city are shown.
