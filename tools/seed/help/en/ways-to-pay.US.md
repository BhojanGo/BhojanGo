In the US, BhojanGo takes payments through Stripe, our payment partner. You pay before the restaurant gets your order, so there's nothing to settle at the door.

## Ways to pay

- **Credit and debit cards.** Your card details go into Stripe's secure form, not ours.
- **Apple Pay**, on devices and browsers that support it.
- **Google Pay**, on devices and browsers that support it.

Checkout shows the ways to pay that work on your device.

## Saved cards

You can save a card to pay faster next time. BhojanGo never sees or stores your full card number: Stripe keeps it and gives us a token, and we show only the card brand, the last 4 digits and the expiry date. You can remove a saved card or choose a default in your account.

## Sales tax

Sales tax is worked out for each order from your delivery address, so your address needs a ZIP code. Tax shows as its own line on your bill.

## If a payment fails

If a payment doesn't go through, you aren't charged. Your order waits, and the price stays held for a while so you can try again with the same or another method. See [Your price is held while you pay](/help/a/price-held-while-you-pay).

## Refunds

Refunds go back to the card or wallet you paid with. See [How do refunds work?](/help/a/how-refunds-work).

> We never ask for your card number or a sign-in code on a call or in a message.
