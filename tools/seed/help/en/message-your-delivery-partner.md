You can message your delivery partner from your order's tracking page, from the moment one is assigned until your order is delivered.

## Quick messages

Tap a ready-made message to send it in one go:

- Please leave it at the door.
- I'm coming down.
- Please ring the bell.
- Please ask the guard at the gate.

Your delivery partner can send you quick messages too, like "I'm at the restaurant, waiting for your order", "Picked up. On my way to you", "I'm outside" or "I can't find the address. Please check the pin on the map".

Quick messages show up in each person's own language, so you can both read them easily.

## Short notes

You can also write a short message of up to 140 characters. It's shown exactly as you wrote it.

## Your privacy

- Nobody sees anyone's phone number. You see your delivery partner's first name, and they see yours.
- If a message contains something that looks like a phone number, the digits are hidden.
- When the order is delivered or cancelled, the conversation closes and is hidden from both of you.

## Tips for a smooth handover

- Make sure the pin on your address is in the right place.
- Add delivery notes, like a gate code or floor, to your saved address.
- Keep your [delivery PIN](/help/a/delivery-pin) ready.

> In an emergency, call your local emergency number. Messages are for the delivery only.
