If you prefer, you can sign in with your email address and a password instead of your phone number.

## Signing in

1. Go to [Sign in](/signin) and choose to sign in with email.
2. Enter your email address and your password.
3. We email you a 6-digit code. Enter it to finish signing in.

The code is an extra check that it's really you, even if someone else knows your password.

## Forgot your password?

1. On the sign-in page, choose **Forgot password**.
2. Enter your email address.
3. We email you a link to set a new password.

The link works **once**, and only for **30 minutes**. If it has expired or you've already used it, ask for a new one.

## Choosing a password

- Use at least **10 characters**.
- We check new passwords against lists of passwords that have leaked in data breaches elsewhere. If yours is on one, we'll ask you to choose another.
- Use a password you don't use anywhere else. A short phrase of a few unrelated words is easy to remember and hard to guess.

## Keep your account safe

- BhojanGo staff will never ask for your password or sign-in code.
- If you get a sign-in code you didn't ask for, someone may know your password. Change it straight away.

> You can still sign in with your phone number and a code by SMS. See [Signing in with your phone number](/help/a/sign-in-with-phone).
