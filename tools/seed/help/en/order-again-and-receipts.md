All your orders live in [Your orders](/account/orders), newest first. You can search by restaurant, dish or order code, filter by in progress, delivered or cancelled, and pick a date range.

## Order again

Open a past order and tap **Order again**. We put the same dishes and options back in your bag so you can check out.

- Dishes are priced at **today's** menu prices, not what you paid last time.
- Dishes the restaurant no longer has, or that are sold out today, are left out.
- Nothing is ordered until you go through checkout. You'll see a fresh bill before you pay.

## Your receipt

Each paid order has a receipt showing the items, the fair bill with who gets each line, the total you paid and how you paid.

You can also download it as a PDF:

- **In India**, it's a tax invoice, with GST and the restaurant's FSSAI licence details.
- **In the US**, it's a receipt showing sales tax.

The PDF has the same lines as the bill you saw at checkout.

## Receipts by email

If you've added an email address to your account, receipts and invoices go there too. You can choose which messages you get in your notification settings.

> Something wrong with a past order? Open it and choose **Report a problem**, within your city's time limit after delivery.
