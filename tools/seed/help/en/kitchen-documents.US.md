In the US, the partner application in the BhojanGo Restaurant app asks for your business details, your menu and a bank account.

## Business details

Your restaurant's name as customers will see it, your street address, and the pin on the map where delivery partners pick up.

## Food permit

The app doesn't ask you to upload a food permit in US cities today, and it shows this step as not needed in your city. You're still responsible for holding the licences and permits your city, county and state require to sell food.

## Tax

You don't need to give us a tax number in the app. Sales tax on each order is worked out from the customer's address and collected by BhojanGo, and it shows as its own line on the customer's bill.

## Bank account

Your bank account is where your weekly payout lands. You set it up securely with our payments partner, Stripe, not by typing your bank details into BhojanGo.

The app shows where your account stands:

- **No bank account yet**: tap **Add bank account** to start.
- **Waiting for your bank details**: finish the set-up with Stripe.
- **Bank account confirmed**: you're ready to be paid.

If your bank isn't confirmed when a payout is due, your statement waits and is paid once you finish setting up.

## Menu

Add at least 5 dishes with prices. Your BhojanGo partner manager helps you set up your menu and photos.

> When every step is done, we review your details and switch your restaurant on. New orders then ring on your BhojanGo Restaurant tablet.
