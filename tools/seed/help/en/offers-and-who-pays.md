Offers lower your bill, and the fair bill shows who is paying for each one.

## Kinds of offers

- **Percent off** your food, sometimes up to a maximum amount.
- **Amount off** your food.
- **Free delivery**, which takes the delivery fee off.
- **First order** offers, for your first BhojanGo order.

Some offers apply only above a minimum food total. Others need a **promo code**, which you enter at checkout.

## Which offer you get

Most offers apply on their own. If more than one food offer fits your order, we give you the one that saves you the most. A free delivery offer can apply on top.

## Who pays

Each offer on your bill is tagged with who funds it:

- **Restaurant pays**: the restaurant is giving you the discount, and it comes out of what the restaurant is paid for the order.
- **BhojanGo pays**: we cover the discount. The restaurant is still paid in full for the food.

## Limits

Offers can have a budget. When an offer's budget for the week, or overall, is used up, it stops. Restaurants choose a weekly limit for the offers they fund.

## Offers and refunds

If you're refunded for a dish that was discounted, you get back what you actually paid for it, after the discount.

> Want to hear about offers? Turn on **Offers** in your notification settings. They're off unless you turn them on.
