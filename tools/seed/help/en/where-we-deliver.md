BhojanGo is live in a growing list of cities in India and the United States. You can see every city, and which ones are coming soon, on our cities page.

## How we decide if we can deliver to you

When you enter your address, we check three things:

1. **Your city.** It needs to be one of our live cities.
2. **Your area.** Each city is split into delivery areas. Your address needs to be inside one.
3. **The distance to the kitchen.** Each city has a limit on how far a kitchen can be from you. You'll only see kitchens close enough to deliver.

If an address is outside our areas, we'll tell you, and show the nearest city we serve if there is one.

## Opening hours

Kitchens set their own hours, and some cities have delivery hours too. If a kitchen is closed, its page tells you why and when it opens next.

## Coming soon

Some cities are marked **Coming soon**. We're getting kitchens and delivery partners ready there.

## Notify me

Not on the list, or coming soon?

1. Enter your city and your email.
2. Tick the box to let us email you.
3. Tap **Notify me**.

We'll email you once, the day BhojanGo starts delivering there, and nothing else. You can ask us to forget your email at any time. The most-requested cities open first, so every sign-up counts.

> Prices, kitchens and languages follow the city you order in. Fees can be different from one city to another.
