The quickest way into BhojanGo is your mobile number. There's no password to remember.

## Signing in

1. Go to [Sign in](/signin), or open the BhojanGo app.
2. Choose your country code and enter your mobile number.
3. Tap **Send code**. We text you a 6-digit code.
4. Enter the code and tap **Verify and continue**.

If your number is new to BhojanGo, this creates your account. If you've ordered before, you're straight back in.

## Didn't get the code?

- Check the number and the country code.
- Wait for the timer, then tap **Resend code**.
- Codes expire after a short while, so use the newest one.
- After too many attempts we stop sending codes to that number for a while, to keep your account safe. Try again later.

## Keep your code to yourself

We'll never ask for your code on a call, and BhojanGo staff don't need it. If anyone asks you for it, don't share it.

## Other ways to sign in

- **Google.** On the website you can also sign in with your Google account, where it's shown.
- **Email and password.** See [Signing in with email and password](/help/a/sign-in-with-email-and-password).

## Changing your number

Your mobile number is how you sign in, so you can't change it yourself. Contact support and we'll help.

> Restaurant partners and delivery partners sign in with their phone number too, in the BhojanGo Restaurant and BhojanGo Delivery apps.
