To go live in India, your kitchen needs three things: an FSSAI licence, a GSTIN and a bank account. You add them in the partner application in the BhojanGo Kitchen app.

## FSSAI food licence

Every kitchen on BhojanGo shows a valid FSSAI licence on its page. You'll need:

- your **licence number**: 14 digits, at the top of your certificate,
- the date it's **valid until**,
- a copy of the **licence**, as a PDF or a photo.

We check the licence before your kitchen goes live.

> If your FSSAI licence expires, your kitchen is paused automatically and customers can't order until you upload the renewed licence. As soon as you add it, your kitchen is listed again.

## GSTIN

Enter your 15-character GSTIN, for example 36AABCU9603R1ZM. Your GSTIN goes on your customers' bills and tax invoices. If you type it wrong, the app asks you to check it.

## Bank account

Your bank account is where your weekly payout lands. You set it up securely with our payments partner, Razorpay, not by typing your bank details into BhojanGo.

The app shows where your account stands:

- **No bank account yet**: tap **Add bank account** to start.
- **Waiting for your bank details**: finish the set-up with our payments partner.
- **Bank account confirmed**: you're ready to be paid.

If your bank isn't confirmed when a payout is due, your statement waits and is paid once you finish setting up.

## Changing your details

You can update your GSTIN or upload a renewed FSSAI licence from the application screen at any time.
