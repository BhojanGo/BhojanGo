When we refund you, the money goes back to the way you paid: the same card, UPI account or other method.

## Refunds for a cancelled order

If a paid order is cancelled, by you, by the kitchen or because the kitchen didn't accept it in time, we refund everything you paid: food, fees, tax and tip.

## Refunds for part of an order

When something is missing, wrong or not good, we refund the items affected, not a rough amount. For each item you get back:

- what you paid for it,
- less its share of any discount on your food,
- plus its share of the tax you paid on food.

So if you paid less for a dish because of an offer, the refund matches what you actually paid. The amounts come from your original bill, so they always add up.

In some cases, such as a delivery problem, our support team can refund the delivery fee and the tax on it too.

## How you get a refund

- **Report it yourself** from the order. Small refunds are made at once; above your city's automatic limit, our support team checks first. See [How do I report a problem?](/help/a/report-a-problem-with-an-order).
- **Cancelled orders** are refunded automatically. You don't need to ask.

## When you'll see the money

We send the refund to your payment provider straight away. How long it takes to show in your account depends on your bank, card issuer or payment app, and can take a few working days.

> We let you know when a refund is made. If a refund hasn't arrived after your bank's usual time, contact support from the order.
