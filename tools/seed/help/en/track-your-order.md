Open your order from [Your orders](/account/orders) to see where it is. The page updates on its own, and the map shows your delivery partner on the way once they have your food.

## The steps you'll see

1. **Waiting for your payment.** We've got your order and are waiting for the payment to be confirmed.
2. **The restaurant has your order.** It's paid and the restaurant has it on their tablet.
3. **The restaurant is cooking.** The restaurant accepted it and is preparing your food.
4. **Finding a delivery partner.** When the food is nearly ready, we offer the trip to delivery partners nearby.
5. **Heading to the restaurant.** A delivery partner accepted and is on the way to pick it up.
6. **Picked up.** Your food is with your delivery partner and on its way to you.
7. **Delivered.** Enjoy your meal.

If an order is cancelled, the page says so. See [Can I cancel my order?](/help/a/cancel-an-order) for why that can happen.

## Your arrival time

The arrival time is worked out from the restaurant's preparation time, the trip from the restaurant to your address and a few minutes to hand over. If a restaurant is very busy, it may add a little extra preparation time, and your estimate moves with it.

## On the same page

- Your **delivery PIN**, to give your delivery partner at the door.
- **Messages** with your delivery partner once one is on the way.
- Your **receipt**, and **Report a problem** once the order is delivered.

> We'll also send updates as your order moves along. You can choose how in your notification settings.
