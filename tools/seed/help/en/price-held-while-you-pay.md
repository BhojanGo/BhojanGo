When you reach checkout, we work out your full bill and hold that exact price for you. Checkout shows how long it's held.

## What's held

Everything on the fair bill: the food, any offer, the fees, the tax and your tip. When you pay, you're charged exactly that total. No surprise changes.

A price is held for 10 minutes.

## If the time runs out

Checkout says **The price has expired**. Tap **Get a fresh price** and we work out the bill again with the current menu, fees and offers. It's often the same, but it can change, for example if a busy-hour fee has started or an offer has ended.

## If your payment fails

If a payment doesn't go through, you aren't charged. Your order waits and the price stays held, so you can try again, with the same method or another one.

If you never complete the payment, the order is cancelled after a while. You aren't charged.

## After you've paid

Your order keeps the bill you paid, and it never changes afterwards. Your receipt and any refunds use those exact amounts.

> Changing your bag, address or tip at checkout gives you a new price, and the hold starts again.
