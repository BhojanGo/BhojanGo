Kitchen partners on BhojanGo start at 0% commission (customers pay BhojanGo a platform fee instead), get paid weekly, and set their own prices and offers. You'll see your terms before you go live.

## Apply

1. Go to [Add your kitchen](/partner) and choose **Start your application**.
2. Fill in **Kitchen name** (as customers will see it), **City**, **Your name**, **Mobile number**, **Email** and a **Password** of at least 10 characters. Add up to 5 cuisines if you like.
3. Choose **Create account and continue**. Your application opens on the same page, where you finish the steps below.

Your kitchen can be in a city we already deliver in, or in one that is coming soon.

> One account is either a customer's or a kitchen partner's. If you're signed in with an account that has never ordered, you can apply with it, and it becomes your kitchen's account. An account that has ordered food can't switch: sign out and use a different email for your kitchen.

## The five steps

After you apply, the app shows your progress:

1. **Business details**: your kitchen's name, address and pin on the map.
2. **Licence**: in India, your FSSAI licence. See [Your kitchen's documents](/help/a/kitchen-documents).
3. **Tax and bank**: in India, your GSTIN; everywhere, the bank account for your weekly payout.
4. **Menu and photos**: at least 5 dishes, with prices. Your BhojanGo partner manager helps you add them.
5. **Tablet and go live**: we check everything and switch your kitchen on.

You can save and come back at any time. The step you're on is highlighted.

## Your terms

The application shows the commission rate for your kitchen: 0% to start. Any commission is a percentage of your food total only, never of packaging, customer fees, tax or tips. Each order's card processing fee comes out of your payout, and your statement shows it order by order.

## Going live

When every step is done, we review your details. Once your kitchen is live, new orders ring on your BhojanGo Kitchen tablet.
