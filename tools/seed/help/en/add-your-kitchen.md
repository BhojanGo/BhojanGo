Kitchen partners on BhojanGo pay commission on food only, get paid weekly, and set their own prices and offers. You'll see your terms before you go live.

## Apply

1. Go to [Add your kitchen](/partner) and choose **Start your application**.
2. Create your account: your email, a password of at least 10 characters, and your kitchen's phone number.
3. Fill in your name, your kitchen's name as customers will see it, your city and up to five cuisines. You can add the full address in the first step after you apply.
4. Send your application.

Your kitchen can be in a city we already deliver in or one that is coming soon.

> Already order food on BhojanGo? Sign in on [Add your kitchen](/partner) and apply with the same account. Later you can sign in with your email and password, or with your phone number.

## The five steps

After you apply, the app shows your progress:

1. **Business details**: your kitchen's name, address and pin on the map.
2. **Licence**: in India, your FSSAI licence. See [Your kitchen's documents](/help/a/kitchen-documents).
3. **Tax and bank**: in India, your GSTIN; everywhere, the bank account for your weekly payout.
4. **Menu and photos**: at least 5 dishes, with prices. Your BhojanGo partner manager helps you add them.
5. **Tablet and go live**: we check everything and switch your kitchen on.

You can save and come back at any time. The step you're on is highlighted.

## Your terms

The application shows the commission rate for your kitchen. It's a percentage of your food total only, never of packaging, customer fees, tax or tips. If your agreement also has a fixed amount per order, your statement shows it.

## Going live

When every step is done, we review your details. Once your kitchen is live, new orders ring on your BhojanGo Kitchen tablet.
