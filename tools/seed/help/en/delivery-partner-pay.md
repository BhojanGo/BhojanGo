You see the whole pay for a trip before you accept it, and the amounts for your city are in the BhojanGo Delivery app before you start.

## Pay for each trip

Each delivery pays:

- a **base** amount for the trip,
- an amount for the **distance** from the kitchen to the customer,
- **waiting pay**, in cities that pay for waiting, when you wait at the kitchen longer than a few free minutes.

In cities where combined trips are switched on, you may be offered two orders from the same kitchen. The second drop-off pays its distance and waiting time plus a bonus for the extra order.

The offer shows this pay as **Guaranteed**, together with the tip, before you accept.

## The minimum hourly pay

Every city has a minimum pay: the least you earn for each active hour.

- **Active time** runs from when you accept an offer to when you drop off. Driving to the kitchen and waiting there count. Overlapping deliveries count once.
- At the end of the week, if your trip pay for your active hours comes to less than the minimum pay, we top it up.
- Tips don't count towards the minimum pay. They're always on top.

The **Earnings** tab shows your week so far, day by day, your pay per active hour and any top-up.

## Tips

Customers can add a tip at checkout. **100% of it is yours.** BhojanGo keeps none of it.

## Weekly payouts

Everything you earn in a week is paid together, to your bank account, through our payments partner: Razorpay in India, Stripe in the US. There's no instant cash-out. The app shows when your next payout is.

If your payout account isn't set up yet, your pay waits and is sent once you finish.

> Past weeks and their statements are under **Account**, then **Payouts**, in the app.
