Here's a delivery from start to finish in the BhojanGo Delivery app.

## Going online

Tap **Go online** on the **Drive** tab. Allow location so we can offer you deliveries nearby. Your location is shared only while you're online. Tap **Go offline** whenever you want to stop.

## Offers

A new offer shows the full pay, including any tip, the distance, the time and where the restaurant is. You only see the area of the drop-off until you pick up. Tap **Accept** before the timer runs out, or **Pass**.

## Pickup

1. Head to the restaurant. The app shows if the food is still being prepared or ready.
2. Check the order code on the bag.
3. Tap **Picked up**. The customer's full address appears now.

## Drop-off

Use **Navigate** to get there. At the door, confirm the delivery one of two ways:

- **Hand over with the PIN**: ask the customer for the 4-digit PIN on their tracking screen and enter it.
- **Photo at the door**: if you leave the order, take a photo of the bags where you leave them.

You can't complete a delivery without one of these.

## Messages

Use quick messages, like "I'm outside", or a short note to reach the customer. Nobody sees anyone's phone number.

## If an order is cancelled

The app tells you the order isn't yours any more. Head back to driving.

## SOS

On every delivery there's an **SOS** button. You'll also find it under **Account**, then **Safety**.

1. If you or anyone else is in danger, call the emergency number first. The app shows it.
2. Then tap **Alert BhojanGo safety team**. They get your alert and your location, and they'll call you.

> Stay somewhere safe after sending an alert. If the alert can't be sent, call your local emergency number.
