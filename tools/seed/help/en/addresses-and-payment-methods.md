Saving your addresses and cards makes checkout quicker. You'll find both under **Profile & settings** in your account.

## Saved addresses

For each address you can add:

- a name, like Home or Work,
- the flat, house and street,
- the city, state and postal code,
- **delivery notes**, like a gate code or which floor,
- **where on the map**: search your area or use your location so your delivery partner can find you.

You can edit or delete an address at any time, and choose one as your **default**. Only one address can be the default.

> A correct map pin matters most. Your delivery partner navigates to the pin, so check it's on your building.

## Saved cards

You can save a credit or debit card when you pay at checkout, or add one from **Payment methods**.

- Your card is entered in our payment partner's secure form: Razorpay in India, Stripe in the US.
- BhojanGo never sees or stores your full card number. We keep only a token from our payment partner, plus the card brand, the last 4 digits and the expiry date.
- You can choose a default card or delete a card at any time.
- A card works until the end of its expiry month.

## Notifications

In **Notifications** you choose how we reach you, by push, SMS or email, for:

- **Order updates**: accepted, on the way, delivered.
- **Receipts and account**: receipts, refunds and sign-in alerts.
- **Offers**: off unless you turn them on.

## Changing your phone number or email

Your mobile number and email are how you sign in, so you can't change them yourself. Contact support and we'll help.
